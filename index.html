      Costs this calculator can't price in

      Ask about these before finalizing any AI-replacement decision. Several have sunk projects that looked good on the spreadsheet.

      • Prompt and integration maintenance every time the underlying model changes underneath you.
      • Customer trust and brand damage when AI errors reach people directly.
      • Compliance and liability exposure for mistakes an experienced employee would have caught.
      • Rehiring and retraining at market rate if the approach doesn't work, usually more than the salary you cut.
      • The undocumented knowledge that leaves with the person: who to call, which exceptions matter, why a rule exists.
      • Morale and attrition among the staff who absorb the transition and watch it happen.
      A planning aid, not a guarantee. Verify current vendor pricing before deciding.
