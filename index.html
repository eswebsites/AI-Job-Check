Costs this calculator can't fully price in

Ask about these before finalizing any AI-replacement decision.

  • Integration and prompt-maintenance time as the underlying model or API changes.
  • Customer trust and brand risk if AI-driven errors reach customers directly.
  • Compliance and liability exposure for mistakes an employee would have caught.
  • Retraining and re-hiring cost if the AI approach doesn't work out and you rehire later.
  • Morale and knowledge loss among remaining staff who absorb the transition.
This is a planning aid, not a guarantee — verify current vendor pricing before deciding.
